Share Directly from Spotify

Set up an Apple Shortcut once (takes 20 seconds). After that, tap Share on any song in Spotify and pick DeckJam!

1. Copy your Personal Quick-Share URL:
https://...
2. Create Shortcut in Apple Shortcuts:
  • Open Shortcuts → Tap + → Name it "Queue in DeckJam".
  • Tap the (i) info icon → Turn on "Show in Share Sheet" (accept URLs & Text).
  • Add action: "URL Encode" (pass Shortcut Input).
  • Add action: "Get Contents of URL" → Paste the copied URL + append the URL Encoded Text variable.
